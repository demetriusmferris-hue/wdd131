A world built

A plan for a fan-made destination celebrating LEGO® building, its history, and the stories behind iconic sets.

01 / Identity

Site name

Brick & Beyond

Brick & Beyond

The name connects the familiar building brick with everything fans can do with it: explore its history, discover remarkable builds, share creations, and see favorite stories brought to life.

Proposed domain: brickandbeyond.com (availability not checked)

This is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the LEGO Group.

02 / What it does

Site purpose

Brick & Beyond will be an inviting, easy-to-browse guide for LEGO fans and curious newcomers. It will share a concise history of the company, spotlight some of the largest and most impressive LEGO builds, and compare selected LEGO Star Wars sets with the vehicles and locations that inspired them in the films. Visitors will also be able to submit photos and descriptions of their own creations through a simple upload form.

03 / Visitor questions

Scenarios

  • How did LEGO grow from a small toy company into a worldwide building brand?
  • What are some of the biggest LEGO builds ever made, and how many pieces do they use?
  • How closely does the LEGO Star Wars X-wing set match the ship I see in the films?
  • Where can I share a photo and details about a LEGO creation I built myself?

04 / Visual system

Color schema

The palette pairs a brick-inspired red and sunny yellow with a deep ink color and a warm, light background. The colors used on this planning page demonstrate the proposed site palette.

Brick red#C92832
Build yellow#F5C842
Deep ink#252A32
Warm cream#FFF9EE
  • Brick red — main headings, links, buttons, and small brand accents.
  • Build yellow — highlighted details, section markers, and calls to action.
  • Deep ink — body copy, navigation, and footer backgrounds for contrast.
  • Warm cream — page background and light content panels.
